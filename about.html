🚚 The Delivery Flow

How Bimbo's Supply Chain Works

From bakery to shelf — here's the journey every bread product takes, and where AI makes the difference.

1

🏭 Bakery Produces

Grupo Bimbo bakes thousands of products daily — bread, cakes, tortillas, snacks — across facilities spanning all of Mexico. Every batch must be delivered before it goes stale.

2

🚚 Depot Distributes

Regional warehouses (Agencias) receive shipments and assign products to specific delivery routes. Each Agencia serves a geographic area of Mexico.

3

🗺️ Route Visits Shops

Each truck (Ruta) follows a fixed weekly route, visiting the same shops on a regular schedule. The driver loads the truck based on expected demand for each stop.

4

🏪 Shop Sells or Returns

Shops sell what they can. Products unsold by the next delivery are returned to the truck — returned products represent direct financial losses. High returns = overstocked. Zero returns but empty shelves = understocked.

AI

🤖 Model Predicts Next Week's Demand

Our Random Forest model analyses this week's sales, returns, and revenue data to predict exactly how many units each shop needs next week — enabling drivers to load their trucks with precision, not guesswork. See how it was built →

⚖️ The Core Challenge

Why Getting It Wrong Is Expensive

Every unit over or under the correct demand is money lost. At Bimbo's scale, small errors add up to enormous costs.

❌ Overstocking Problems
  • 🗑️ Bread goes stale and must be returned — direct product loss
  • 💸 Returns must be transported back, wasted truck capacity
  • 📦 Overstocked shelves crowd out other products
  • 📉 Return rates inflate operational costs company-wide
  • ⚠️ Too many returns can damage supplier relationships
✅ What AI Fixes
  • 🎯 Predicts exact demand per product per shop per week
  • 📊 Uses 97% signal from current week's sales as primary input
  • 🌲 30-tree Random Forest ensemble for robust predictions
  • 📉 Log-transforms skewed demand for learnable distribution
  • 🚀 Generates 7M+ predictions in a single inference run
