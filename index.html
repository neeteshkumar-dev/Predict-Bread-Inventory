⚙️ The Problem We Solve

How Grupo Bimbo's Supply Chain Works

Mexico's largest bakery delivers fresh products to thousands of shops weekly. Too much = stale returns. Too little = lost sales. Our AI finds the perfect quantity. Learn the full story →

🏭

Bakery Produces

Grupo Bimbo bakes thousands of products daily — bread, cakes, snacks — across facilities throughout Mexico.

🚚

Truck Delivers

Each delivery truck follows a fixed route and visits shops weekly, dropping off precisely stocked fresh product.

🏪

Shop Sells

Shops sell what they can. Anything unsold or stale gets returned to the truck the following week at a cost.

🤖

AI Predicts

Our model predicts exact demand per product per shop — so the truck brings precisely the right amount.

📉

Zero Waste

Less overstock means less waste, lower costs, fresher products, and materially higher profit margins.

🌲 The Model

Random Forest — 30 Trees Deep

An ensemble of 30 decision trees, each trained on a random sample of 240,000 delivery records. The forest votes collectively to produce a robust prediction that no single tree could achieve.

  • Trained on 240K weekly delivery records
  • Log-transformed target for skewed distributions
  • n_jobs=-1 parallel training for maximum speed
  • Evaluated via RMSLE — the official Kaggle metric
